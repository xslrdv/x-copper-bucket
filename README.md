# 🪣 X Copper Bucket

A vanilla-friendly **Minecraft** mod for **NeoForge** that introduces balanced *Copper Buckets* for early-game fluid and mob handling without undermining the importance of the classic *Iron Bucket*.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Copper Bucket**
  * Crafted using **3** *Copper Ingots* in a standard bucket shape.
* **Fluids & Blocks**
  * **Copper Water Bucket**: Scoop up and place water sources early in your world.
  * **Copper Milk Bucket**: Collect milk from *cows*, *mooshrooms*, and *goats* to clear status effects.
  * **Copper Powder Snow Bucket**: Gather and place powder snow blocks safely.
* **Bucketable Mobs**
  * Catch, transport, and release aquatic mobs (*Cod*, *Salmon*, *Tropical Fish*, *Pufferfish*, *Axolotl*, *Tadpole*).
  * As well as *Sulfur Cubes*.
* **Balanced Heat Limit**
  * Due to copper's lower melting point, **Copper Buckets** *CANNOT* hold *lava*-keeping the vanilla *Iron Bucket* essential for Nether portals and high-temperature tasks.

---

## 💡 Why Copper Bucket?

* **Balanced Progression**: Gives copper an immediate practical use before you have surplus iron, while preserving the value of the vanilla *Iron Bucket*.
* **Vanilla-Friendly**: Cleanly integrates into standard Minecraft survival mechanics without unnecessary clutter or power creep.

---

## 🛠️ Installation & Requirements

### Requirements
1. **Minecraft**: `26.3`
2. Mod Loader: **NeoForge** `26.3.0.95+`

### Installation
1. Download and install **NeoForge** (version `26.3.0.95` or higher) for **Minecraft** `26.3`.
2. Download the latest release of **X Copper Bucket**.
3. Place the `.jar` file into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and enjoy!
